XAMP 2 iOS 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 3 日 · 開發者：ZHENG-FENG LIN

本機音樂與偏好

XAMP 2 是本機音樂播放器。匯入的音樂、歌曲標籤、封面、波形、播放清單、喜歡的歌曲與介面偏好保存在 App 內。音訊解碼、PCM → DSD 轉換與相關處理在裝置上執行。

資料收集與追蹤

XAMP 2 不需要註冊開發者帳號，沒有廣告、追蹤或分析 SDK，也沒有開發者營運的雲端音樂庫或 AI 服務。App 不會自動把你的音樂、音樂庫或使用資料傳送到開發者伺服器或第三方 AI 服務。

歌唱模式與錄音

你主動製作伴奏時，內建模型在裝置上分離人聲與伴奏，不上傳音訊，也不連線至 AI 服務。產物保存在本機快取，可自行移除，系統亦可能清除。只有你允許麥克風並開始錄音後才收音；暫停、切歌或離開 App 會結束錄音。混音錄音保存在本機 Documents 的 Karaoke Recordings，你可透過「檔案」刪除，或使用系統分享匯出給選定對象。App 不會自動分享或上傳錄音。日文假名與中文繁體轉換也在本機執行。

你主動搜尋的網路歌詞

只有按下搜尋時，App 才把填寫的歌名、演出者及歌曲長度傳送至所選的網易雲音樂、酷狗或 LRCLIB；自動模式會查詢多個來源。搜尋時會讀取少量候選歌詞以優先顯示逐字版本；預覽會重用已讀內容或要求指定版本。服務也會收到連線必要資訊，例如 IP 位址，並依各自政策處理。App 不上傳音訊、封面、檔案路徑或整個曲庫，不登入這些服務，也不保存其 Cookie。確認套用的歌詞及來源保存在本機；未搜尋時不連線查詞。

系統檔案與播放服務

系統檔案選擇器只提供你明確授權選取的檔案，匯入後會在 App 內保存副本。為提供背景播放與系統控制，App 會將目前歌曲名稱、演出者、專輯、封面、進度與播放狀態提供給 iOS 的 Now Playing 介面。

你選用的 iCloud 或其他檔案供應商、iOS 系統備份與 Apple 診斷功能，依各服務的設定及政策處理資料。這些系統功能不等同 XAMP 開發者收集資料。你主動開啟的 GitHub 支援連結或本網站，亦適用相關平台的隱私政策。

保存與刪除

本機資料保留在 App 內，直到你移除 App 或透過系統刪除 App 資料。移除 App 會一併刪除 App 內的音樂副本、音樂庫與偏好；請保留原始音樂檔案。系統備份的保留或刪除由你的系統設定控制。

聯絡與問題回報

可至 XAMP GitHub 問題回報頁聯絡開發者。請避免公開貼上私人音樂檔、個人資料或完整裝置紀錄。App 不會自動替你附加這些資料。

XAMP 2 for iOS — Privacy Policy

Updated November 3, 2026 · Developer: ZHENG-FENG LIN

Local music and settings

Imported audio, track tags, artwork, waveforms, playlists, favorites and preferences are stored in the app. Audio decoding, PCM-to-DSD conversion and related processing run on your device.

No developer collection or tracking

XAMP 2 requires no developer account and includes no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. There is no developer-operated cloud library or AI service. The app does not automatically send your music, library or usage data to developer servers or third-party AI services.

Singing mode and recordings

When you request accompaniment, the bundled model separates vocals and accompaniment on your device without uploading audio or contacting an AI service. Results are cached locally and may be removed by you or the system. Microphone capture starts only after permission and an explicit recording action. Pausing, changing tracks or leaving the app ends recording. Mixed recordings remain in Documents/Karaoke Recordings; you can delete them through Files or explicitly export them using system sharing. Recordings are never automatically shared or uploaded. Japanese readings and Traditional Chinese conversion also run locally.

Lyrics searches you initiate

Only an explicit search sends the entered title, artist and song duration to the selected NetEase, Kugou or LRCLIB provider; automatic mode queries multiple providers. A few candidate lyrics are fetched to prioritize word-timed results. Previews reuse fetched content or request the selected version. Providers receive necessary connection information such as your IP address and handle requests under their own policies. The app does not upload audio, artwork, file paths or your entire library, sign in to those services or retain their cookies. Lyrics you apply and their source are stored locally. No lyrics requests are made when you do not search.

System services

The system document picker grants access to the files you select. The app imports its own copies. To support background playback and system controls, it provides the current track title, artist, album, artwork, progress and playback state to the iOS Now Playing interface.

Your selected file providers, iCloud, system backups and Apple diagnostics operate according to their own settings and policies. External support links and this website are also subject to the relevant platform's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local copies and settings remain in the app until you delete the app or its data using system controls. Deleting the app removes those local copies; keep your original music files. Backup retention and deletion depend on your system settings.

Contact

Contact the developer through XAMP GitHub Issues. Avoid posting private audio, personal information or full device logs publicly. The app does not attach such data automatically.
